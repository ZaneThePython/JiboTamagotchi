# Tamagotchi

A little pet lives on Jibo's screen. Feed it, play with it and put it to bed, and it grows from an egg into a baby, a kid and finally a grown-up with its own antenna.

## How it works

1. Open **Tamagotchi** from Jibo's main menu. The first time, you get an egg that hatches after about a minute.
2. Watch its three needs on the right: **Food**, **Fun** and **Energy**. They slowly go down over time, even while the skill is closed.
3. Tap **Feed** to fill its tummy. Don't overdo it, or it gets too full and grumpy.
4. Tap **Play** to cheer it up. Playing makes it a little hungry and tired.
5. Tap **Sleep** to put it to bed. It recovers energy while asleep and wakes up on its own once rested, or tap **Wake** to wake it early.
6. Swipe down to leave. Your pet is saved and keeps living until you come back.

## Moods

- **Happy:** all needs are well looked after. It bounces, blushes and smiles.
- **Okay:** something is getting low. It bobs gently with a straight face.
- **Sad:** a need is almost empty and its bar turns red. It droops and looks worried.
- **Asleep:** eyes closed, slow breathing and a few Zs.
- **Gone:** gray with X eyes after 4 days without a visit.

If you don't open Tamagotchi for 4 days, your pet dies. Tap any button to start over with a new egg.

## Growing up

- **Egg:** the first minute.
- **Baby:** the first day.
- **Kid:** days two and three.
- **Grown-up:** from day four, bigger and with an antenna.

## Requirements

5x1 3.1.7 or newer. Works offline.
